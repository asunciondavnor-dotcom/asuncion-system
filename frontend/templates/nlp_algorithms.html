How to read the metrics
Accuracy
The percentage of validation pairs assigned their expected class.
Precision
Of pairs predicted in a class, the percentage that belong to it.
Recall
Of all pairs in a class, the percentage the model found.
F1
The harmonic mean of precision and recall. A low value in either reduces F1.
Macro average
Each of the four classes counts equally in the average.
Prediction confidence
The model’s score for one pair. Reviewers confirm the actual requirements and findings from the source documents.
